Description
The San Diego State University School of Communication seeks to hire a tenure - track assistant professor
with expertise in communication theory and methods . The successful candidate is expected to have a
strong program of communication scholarship grounded in communication theory and methods , with
applications across instructional , interpersonal , intercultural , organizational , health , environmental , or
digital communication that support a rigorous and impactful research agenda . The faculty member will be
expected to teach and serve as the coordinator for three foundational undergraduate courses :
communication theory ( COMM 300 – Conceptualizing Communication ), communication methods ( COMM
350 – Investigating Communication ), and the culminating undergraduate capstone course ( COMM 495 –
Communication Capstone ). At the graduate level , they will teach foundational courses in communication
theory ( COMM 610 ) and methods ( COMM 602 ). Apply via PageUp by October 16 , 2026 .
The School of Communication envisions sustained excellence in advancing communication scholarship ,
promoting social justice , fostering relationships , and enhancing the public good . We enact this vision
through inspired teaching , innovative collaborative research , and impactful community engagement . The
School of Communication is looking for a candidate who complements our existing disciplinary diversity
spanning organizational , health , interpersonal / relational , rhetorical , intercultural , digital , and critical -
cultural communication studies in a collegial environment that supports interdisciplinary collaboration . To
learn more , please visit https :// communication . sdsu . edu /.
